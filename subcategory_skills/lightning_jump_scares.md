# Subcategory Skill: lightning_jump_scares (Niche: nature_weather)

## Overview & Narrative Structure
`lightning_jump_scares` focuses on sudden, shocking transitions from tranquil atmospheric weather setups (peaceful rain listening, filming rolling storms from a porch, casual dock conversation) into blinding, violent ground-level lightning strikes with genuine shock reactions and camera drops.

Every clip strictly follows the **3-Beat Dynamic Arc**:
1. **Tranquil Buildup (3.0s - 4.5s)**: Rain pitter-patter, calm skies or dark distant clouds, human calmness.
2. **Blinding Climax Strike (0.5s - 1.0s)**: Sudden instantaneous frame whiteout/purple flash, explosive sonic crack, debris or fence spark.
3. **Shock Reaction / Camera Drop (2.0s - 3.0s)**: Shaky drop into floor/grass, audible gasp/scream, sprint indoors for shelter.
