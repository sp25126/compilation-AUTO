# Universal Situation Evaluator Skill (50 Sub-Categories)

## Operational Rules
1. Never approve clips based only on title matches. Verify the primary subject and physical action.
2. Reject any video containing:
   - Talking heads, voiceover reactions, podcast mics, or split-screen reacts.
   - Screen recordings, video game HUDs (Roblox, GTA, Valorant), CGI/3D animations, or cartoons.
   - Compilations with pre-burned leaderboards, captions, or watermarks.
   - Staged Public Service Announcements (PSAs) or static dashcam traffic footage without an incident.

---

## The 10 Niche Taxonomy & Visual Signatures

### 1. Human Physical Fails (`human_fails`)
- **People Accepting Fate:** Clear moment of surrender where a person stops struggling mid-fall, slide, or descent.
- **Battling Gravity & Losing:** Physical balance struggle on ladders, roofs, trampolines, ice, or hammock flips.
- **Clumsy Public Moments:** Tripping, dropping items, or walking into glass doors in public settings.
- **Overconfident Humbled:** Stunts (skate, parkour, gymnastics) failing immediately after a boast or setup.
- **Moments Before Impact:** Fast-moving objects or collisions frozen at the point of realization.

### 2. Instant Regret & Karma (`instant_karma`)
- **Defying Warning Signs:** Person physically trespassing or ignoring safety barriers (thin ice, wet paint, danger signs).
- **Pranks Backfired:** Prankster caught or countered by their target.
- **Taunting Animals:** Person provoking an animal (goose, ram, dog) and getting charged.
- **Road Rage Justice:** Retaliation, aggressive cut-offs resulting in spins, or marked police cruisers intervening.
- **Cocky Challengers:** Early celebrators or showboaters getting instantly countered.

### 3. Funny Animals & Pets (`funny_animals`)
- **Pets Being Dumb:** Real pets miscalculating jumps, getting stuck in boxes, running into sliding glass.
- **Pets Being Smart:** Animals opening doors, unlocking latches, or manipulating tools.
- **Animal Funny Moments:** Unpredictable domestic pet chaos and zoomie wipeouts.
- **Pets In Slow Motion:** Facial distortion and awkward paws during mid-air leaps.
- **Animals Becoming Scarier:** Sudden stare-downs, hind-leg walks, or uncharacteristic territorial shifts.

### 4. Infinite Aura Loss (`aura_debt`)
- **-1,000,000 Aura Moments:** Dramatic drop in dignity (slipping while attempting a cool entrance).
- **Failed Swagger & Poses:** Sunglasses falling, slipping out of sports cars, missed high-fives.
- **Left Hanging In Public:** Handshakes ignored on broadcast television or stages.
- **Luxury Flexes Backfiring:** High-end gear or car stunts failing on camera.
- **Adults Humbled By Kids:** Adults losing sports challenges or physical games to young children.

### 5. Sports & Athletic Bloopers (`sports_bloopers`)
- **Athletes Celebrating Too Early:** Taunting or slowing down before the finish line, resulting in defeat.
- **Trickshot Disasters:** Trickshots breaking home furniture, lighting, or windows.
- **Goalkeeper Brainfades:** Goalies miskicking or dropping routine catches into the net.
- **Gymnast Routine Mishaps:** Balance beam or vault landing slides into mats.
- **Referee Collisions:** Referees physically obstructing players or getting knocked down.

### 6. Real-Life Glitches & NPCs (`npc_glitches`)
- **Unrendered Game NPCs:** Strange static T-poses, blank stares, repetitive public actions.
- **Dialogue Loop Real Life:** People repeating gestures or phrases compulsively.
- **Pathfinding AI Broken:** Walking repeatedly into obstacles or glass doors.
- **Object Collision Fails:** Dropping items awkwardly as if hand collision failed.
- **NPC Idle Animations:** Synchronized unacquainted strangers moving in cadence.

### 7. Workplace Disasters (`workplace_disasters`)
- **Worst Shift Ever:** Spilling bulk paint, milk, flour, or commercial inventory.
- **Forklift Domino Disasters:** Racks collapsing following light forklift or pallet impact.
- **Commercial Kitchen Nightmares:** Dropping massive soup vats or multi-tier wedding cakes.
- **Retail Display Rampage:** Knocking over display pyramids in stores.
- **Delivery Driver Mishaps:** Packages sliding down icy paths or steps.

### 8. Extreme Luck & Saves (`extreme_luck`)
- **Used All Luck For Life:** Close-call evasion of falling objects or vehicles by inches.
- **Accidental Trickshots:** Dropped items bouncing into cups or upright positions.
- **Physics-Defying Saves:** Ejected riders landing on feet.
- **Unintentional Hero Catches:** Blind catches of falling infants, phones, or beverages.
- **Vehicle 360 Recoveries:** Complete highway spinouts continuing in lane unharmed.

### 9. Gym & Fitness Bloopers (`gym_bloopers`)
- **Bench Press Ego Fails:** Overloaded bars pinning lifters or dumping plates.
- **Treadmill Slingshots:** Excessive treadmill speed launching users back.
- **Squat Rack Blunders:** Lifters unable to stand up, dumping the bar on safeties.
- **Equipment Misuse:** Inverted cable usage or using machines backwards.
- **Spotter Distracted:** Spotters looking away while lifters miss racks.

### 10. Nature & Weather Surprises (`nature_weather`)
- **Sudden Wind Ruining Events:** Canopies, bounce houses, or umbrellas blown away.
- **Rogue Waves:** Shoreline surges washing beach chairs and towels out.
- **Ice Driveway Slides:** Uncontrollable slides down frozen driveways.
- **Wildlife Close Calls:** Animals approaching outdoor tourists or campsites.
- **Lightning Jump Scares:** Nearby strikes captured on home security or phone video.
